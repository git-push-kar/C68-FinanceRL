About this portfolio & research evidence

This portfolio replays the fixed InternVL3-2B finance adapter over 2 February–30 October 2026. Its learned strategy preferences blend five price-based allocation rules. Allocation targets change with lagged market data; the model is not reading news or forecasting prices. Rebalances occur every five sessions, with 0.10% fees on purchases plus sales.

Seed 42 was fixed before the final evaluation. The three trained seeds improve Sharpe and reduce drawdown versus the matched original model, with slightly lower total return. The standalone minimum-variance strategy is stronger. The final 95% interval for mean Sharpe improvement includes zero; this is promising, not conclusive, evidence.

Sharpe uses 252 sessions/year and a zero risk-free rate. No slippage, market impact, taxes or broker execution are modeled. Recorded weights are adjusted-price exposures, not share lots. The portfolio replays verified outputs from the frozen adapter and exact execution ledger. Questions invoke InternVL3-2B with a separately trained finance explanation adapter, those records, and conversation history. The original RL adapter remains frozen for the portfolio decisions. Generated explanations are not evidence of its internal thought process.
