About this demonstration & research results

The selected Nifty strategy-preference RL adapter (seed42, checkpoint44) is called on each five-session rebalance. Its learned preferences blend equal weight, inverse volatility, momentum and reversal. The preferences are fixed; strategy targets change using prices available one close before execution. There is no news-based decision making or live broker connection.

Training: 2019–2021. Validation: 2022. Retrospective continuous test: 1 January 2025–30 January 2026. Untrained return 11.22%, trained 13.28%, equal-weight buy-and-hold 13.06%. Rebalanced equal weight returned 13.44%. The small advantage over holding is uncertain and cost-sensitive. Historical data had previously been accessed during this research.

Trading fees are 0.10% of amounts bought plus sold. Prices are vendor-adjusted; dividends are reflected in them. Slippage, market impact and taxes are not modeled. Values represent fractional adjusted-price exposures, not executable whole-share orders. Untrained and holding lines are reference calculations; the trained path uses actual adapter inference.

The decision inspector shows execution facts and the allocation calculation. It is not a generated explanation or a transcript of the model’s thoughts.

Historical paper trading · The chart reveals only the sessions already advanced.
